Apple Calendar showing duplicates or stale items?

Apple Calendar caches subscribed feeds and sometimes adds a fresh subscription every time you tap the Subscribe link, so duplicates and ghost entries can build up.

Fix:

  1. In Apple Calendar, delete every "Compass" subscription you can find (Mac: Calendar → Settings → Accounts; iOS: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Subscribed).
  2. Hit Regenerate link below — that 404s any zombie subscriptions still pointing at the old URL.
  3. Tap the new Subscribe link above once.
